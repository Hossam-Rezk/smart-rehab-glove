How to use

1. Connect
Plug in the USB cable and press Connect glove at the top.
2. Calibrate
Teach the glove what a flat and a fully bent finger look like.
3. Exercise
Do Finger Bend and Thumb Touch. The AI checks every repetition.

Calibrate the glove

Do this every time you put the glove on. It takes about 8 seconds.

1
Keep the finger flat
After 1 beep, keep the middle finger straight for 3 seconds.
2
Bend the finger fully
After 2 beeps, bend it as far as you can and hold for 3 seconds.
3
Done
3 beeps: calibration finished. Relax the hand.
